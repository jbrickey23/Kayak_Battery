# Kayak_Battery — Decision Log
Newest first. APPROVED design direction is NOT a fabricated or validated assembly.

## KBAT-DEC-008 — Test a recessed cassette within longitudinal rails
- **Status:** APPROVED INVESTIGATION.
- Investigate positioning battery cell modules between/below 20x20 rails rather than on top; recover effective battery height. Do not infer a complete pack fits from loose cells alone.

## KBAT-DEC-007 — Prototype six hump-conforming saddles
- **Status:** PREFERRED PROTOTYPE, NOT FINAL.
- Two saddles at each F/C/R support station under two fore/aft 20x20 rails. Use contoured PVC pipe sections or custom ABS, removable closed-cell foam, and *two fore/aft-spaced countersunk bolts per saddle* into extrusion T-nuts. Trial fit decides foam and material. Transverse ABS ribs remain fallback.

## KBAT-DEC-006 — Geometry capture and pivot-lock deck struts
- **Status:** APPROVED CONCEPT; LOAD PATH UNVERIFIED.
- Four fixed-length, two-bolt, swing-up padded struts to underside upper deck as anti-lift restraint; padded fore/aft geometry stops against suitable hull structures. Avoid deck jacking, weak scupper contact, holes and gratuitous adjusters. Adhesive/tape only for positioning pads/components, not retention.

## KBAT-DEC-005 — Three support stations
- **Status:** FIXED DESIGN REQUIREMENT.
- Forward / center / rear stations retained even if additional support; cassette form unknown.

## KBAT-DEC-004 — Hull-supported weight, hard ceiling
- **Status:** FIXED DESIGN REQUIREMENT.
- Load travels into hull hump/appropriate structural surfaces through replaceable compliant pads. Hatch flange does not support pack weight. No structure may extend above existing flange top; preserve drainage.

## KBAT-DEC-003 — Battery-agnostic 2020 rail framework
- **Status:** APPROVED DIRECTION.
- Two longitudinal 20x20 aluminum T-slot rails with removable connections for different battery cassettes; rail/saddle dimensions still to be verified.

## KBAT-DEC-002 — 40% energy gain benchmark and net-weight criterion
- **Status:** FIXED DESIGN GOAL / BENCHMARK.
- Aim for substantially more capacity than 200Ah reference (~40% uplift to 280Ah nominal was prior comparison) while minimizing installed weight. Include weight of separate ~50Ah electronics battery potentially removed. Previous 2–3lb estimate not a verified full system delta.

## KBAT-DEC-001 — No premature cell or motor decisions
- **Status:** FIXED PROCESS RULE.
- No chosen cell count, 4S configuration, battery maker, BMS, module form, or new motor voltage. First prove actual 3D volume and support approach, then choose electrical design using manufacturer verified dimensions and constraints.
